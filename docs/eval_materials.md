## What this cannot show

n = 30 is small: one or two items flip a percentage by several points, so this is not a general accuracy claim. The sample is stratified (near equal per group), so the majority-class baseline here is much lower than it would be on a real shopping mix, where cotton dominates. The products come from a small set of mostly US direct-to-consumer brands on Shopify, with their own photo styles. The groups are coarse and the labels are fiber-based, so an acrylic sweater counts as synthetic even when it looks like wool.
